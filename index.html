High-Level Architecture

"Accept quickly, process carefully, store reliably, and expose clearly."

1️⃣

Client Access

The entry point used by the client application or business consumer — submit requests, receive acknowledgement and notifications, and consult results.

2️⃣

Search API

The front door of the platform — receives, validates, and standardizes each request, confirming acceptance immediately.

3️⃣

Processing Orchestration

Moves requests into background processing, protecting the platform from delays and supporting scalability as demand grows.

4️⃣

Internet Search & Content Preparation

Performs the actual research and refinement — searching, deduplicating, validating, and classifying content into a curated result set.

5️⃣

Information Repository

The long-term knowledge store — keeps requests and results, preserves traceability, and supports consultation and reporting.

6️⃣

Notification Capability

Informs interested consumers when requested information becomes available, avoiding constant manual status checking.

7️⃣

MCP Access Layer

Exposes curated information in a structured way, enabling reuse by AI-enabled or MCP-compatible consumers.

Architecture Principles

Well-established principles appropriate for client-facing enterprise platforms

Separation of Responsibilities

Each major capability has a clear role: receiving requests, processing information, storing results, notifying users, and exposing curated data.

Responsiveness

The platform answers quickly at the start of the process, while the more time-consuming research activities happen in the background.

Scalability

The design allows the search workload to grow independently from the client-facing entry point.

Resilience

External search providers may be slower or temporarily unavailable. The architecture reduces the impact of those variations on the client experience.

Reusability

Processed information is stored and made available for later consultation, rather than being treated as a disposable response.

Controlled Exposure

The MCP layer provides a clear and governed way to access stored information without coupling consumers to the internal processing flow.

Security and Governance

Modern architecture best practices, even at a high level

🔐 Secure Access

  • Only authorized consumers should be allowed to submit or consult requests
  • Access should be governed according to the client and business context

🛡️ Data Protection

  • Stored information should be protected appropriately
  • Sensitive credentials and access secrets must remain controlled
  • Auditability should be maintained for important actions and lifecycle events

📊 Operational Control

  • The platform should monitor search activity, status, throughput, and failures
  • Usage controls should prevent abuse and help protect service quality

✅ Quality & Trustworthiness

  • Collected information should be validated and curated before being presented as available business data
  • Duplicated or low-quality entries should be reduced whenever possible
